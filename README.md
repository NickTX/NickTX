### Nick Golde

I'm an infrastructure engineer and manager, based in Austin. I like building the teams and tech that makes everything else work: cloud environments, automation, deploy pipelines, and the glue code that gets different systems talking to each other. Databases are a big part of that too, and my recent projects are using AI to untangle legacy data pipelines, modernize old code, and document business critical logic/infrastructure. I'm interested in the practical side: where AI saves real time, and how a small team can get a lot more done with the right tools.

Earlier in my career I was an early infrastructure hire at WordStream and helped take it from a small startup to its acquisition by Gannett.

What you'll find here:

Small tools and scripts I've written to scratch an itch
Integrations and automation experiments
Side projects, built on my own time

Easiest place to reach me is LinkedIn.
